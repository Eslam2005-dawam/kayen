## Project summary (from تخيل boards)

**Product:** Arabic RTL e-commerce for mattresses, bedding, pillows, protectors, and accessories — **Saudi market** (prices in **SAR**).

**Brand feel:** Premium sleep / hotel comfort; trustworthy and calm (not discount-heavy unless promo).

**Visual identity (consistent across boards):**

| Element | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| Primary | Deep burgundy / maroon (`~#5C161E`–`#800020`) |
| Background | Cream, beige, white |
| Typography | Modern Arabic sans-serif + English “KAYAN MATTRESS” wordmark |
| Logo | Stylized wing/leaf mark + “KAYAN MATTRESS” |
| Photography | Warm bedroom lifestyle, product cutaways/layers where relevant |
| UI | Rounded CTAs, product cards with shadow, icon trust strip |

**Taglines seen on boards (pick one for build):**

- أكثر من مرتبة… غداً أفضل / Better Sleep • Better Life  
- راحة .. جودة .. حياة أفضل  
- SLEEP BETTER. LIVE BETTER.

**Core user journey:**

`Home → Shop/Categories → Product detail → Cart → Checkout → Order confirmation`

**Homepage sections (from wireframes — baseline for your upcoming home prompt):**

1. Header: logo, nav (الرئيسية، المنتجات، من نحن، …), search, account, cart  
2. Hero: headline + lifestyle image + primary CTA (تسوق الآن / اكتشف المجموعة)  
3. Category shortcuts (مراتب، مخدات، واقيات، مفروشات، …) — icons or cards  
4. Best sellers / new collection product grid or carousel  
5. “Why Kayan?” trust row (توصيل سريع، دفع آمن، جودة، ضمان، …)  
6. Optional: promo strip, newsletter, footer with policies + contact  

**Other planned pages (later):** category PLP with filters, PDP (size/firmness, tabs, reviews), cart, checkout (Mada/Visa/COD/Apple Pay), account, about, contact + map, FAQ, policies, mattress guide.

## External reference stores (design & UX patterns)

Use these for **layout, merchandising, and Arabic e-commerce conventions** — not to copy branding verbatim.

### 1. [Yafet Egypt](https://yafetegypt.com/)

- **Category:** Premium Egyptian cotton bedding (sheets, duvets, towels).  
- **Borrow:** Heritage storytelling (“Since 1964”), editorial hero lines, **color swatches on PLP**, “Quick Add”, clean Shopify-style product cards, understated luxury.  
- **Tone:** Nostalgic + quality craft; good reference for **hero copy** and bestseller section.

### 2. [Magic Potion](https://magicpotion.sa/)

- **Category:** Luxury perfumes (Salla).  
- **Borrow:** **Latest / bestsellers** blocks, **FAQ accordion**, **customer reviews** carousel, trust bar (دفع آمن، توصيل سريع، …), **WhatsApp (Selia)** support pattern, rich footer story block.  
- **Tone:** High-end Arabic copy; strong **section rhythm** on homepage.

### 3. [Royal EG](https://royal-eg.store/)

- **Category:** Duvets, covers, sheets (EGP).  
- **Borrow:** **Category hub** (“Shop by category”), **seasonal collection** hero, **#1 this week** social proof, **quick view**, compare-at pricing, **delivery/COD/exchange** micro-copy under grids.  
- **Tone:** Hotel comfort at home — **closest narrative match** to Kayan mattress positioning.

### 4. [Nomaah](https://nomaah.com/)

- **Category:** Mattresses & hotel bedding (KSA, Salla).  
- **Borrow:** **Category grid**, many **curated collections** (named rails), national-day **announcement bar**, reviews wall, trust icons — **direct competitor patterns** for Kayan.  
- **Tone:** Playful section titles + serious product breadth.

### 5. [Al Sultan KSA](https://alsultan-ksa.com/)

- **Category:** Bed linen & home textile (KSA).  
- **Borrow:** **Promo ticker** (shipping + coupon), **price-led collections** (“170 SAR and less”), category chips, FAQ + reviews + **4-column trust footer**.  
- **Tone:** Value + volume; good for **promo strip** and category merchandising.

### 6. [Merve](https://merve.sa/)

- **Category:** Mattresses, linen, towels (KSA).  
- **Borrow:** **Free-shipping threshold banner**, category browse grid, **brand story** block, seasonal splits (summer/winter), **branch/locations** section if needed.  
- **Tone:** Saudi brand, structured homepage similar to Nomaah but cleaner hierarchy.

### 7. [Livora Scents](https://www.livorascents.com/)

- **Category:** Home fragrance (EN-first).  
- **Borrow:** **Founder note**, emotional scent storytelling, **quiz/ discovery CTA**, flagship product spotlight, “trusted by brands” — adapt as **founder/quality story** or **sleep quiz / mattress finder** later.  
- **Tone:** Refined minimal luxury; good for **white space** and editorial blocks on home.
